# Internal Release v0.1.8

## Paper Mario Style, First-Person Spyglass & Stargazing Loop

### Visual & Environmental Overhaul (Paper Mario Aesthetic)
- Stripped heavy bloom, depth of field, and sun rays from Lighting.
- Added clean warm lighting with custom `PaperGrade` ColorCorrection.
- Changed 170+ environment parts to SmoothPlastic with soft paper-matte finishes, removing glossy metal reflections and eye-straining neons.
- Locked StarterPlayer camera to first-person (`CameraMode.LockFirstPerson`).

### Stargazing Core Gameplay (Replacing Button Clicking / Meteor Catching)
- Placed interactive 3D Brass Spyglass on the Lobby lectern.
- Aim mode activated by holding/toggling Right Mouse Button (RMB) with smooth optic zoom and breath steadying (`Shift`).
- Star species glide through the night sky with animated 2D sticker billboards.
- Players aim and left-click (`LMB`) to capture passing stars, yielding starlight multiplied by their research rating.
- Replaced manual +1 clicks with full server-authoritative raycast shot resolution (`ServerScriptService.StarGazing`).

### Star Catalog & Rating System (F to S+)
- 9 distinct star species ranging from Common (Dim Drifter) to Legendary (Aurora Serpent).
- Progressive mastery tiers: F, E, D, C, B, A, S, S+.
- Repeated captures of a star species elevate its tier, unlocking permanent multiplier bonuses on subsequent catches.
- Full Star Catalog collection page with sticker cards, rarity tags, progress bars, and rank badges.

### Paper Mario UI System
- Replaced previous HUD and dashboard with modular paper-craft UI kit (`ReplicatedStorage.PaperTheme` and `ReplicatedStorage.UIKit`).
- Features thick ink strokes, offset drop-shadow cards, bouncy sticker headers, and bold tactile buttons.
- Free-cursor modal interaction while in first-person mode so full dashboard navigation works seamlessly.

### Pacing, Unlocks & Guided Tutorial
- Added step-by-step onboarding sequence (`StarterGui.ObservatoryHUD.Tutorial`):
  1. Pick up the spyglass from the lectern.
  2. Raise the spyglass.
  3. Spot and shoot a first star.
  4. Open the Star Catalog menu.
  5. Purchase first lens upgrade.
  6. Accumulate starlight for the Orrery Wing door.
- Slowed room gating pacing; door signs now explicitly display real-time starlight progress (`500 Starlight required`).
- Added cinematic camera pan and unsealing sequence when gates unlock so the player clearly understands world progression.
