# Internal Release v0.1.2

## Floor Z-Fighting Fix & Astrolabe Refinement

### Summary of Deliverables
- Floor Architecture Fix:
  - Eliminated severe coplanar Z-fighting in the main observatory atrium where `AtriumFloor` and `AtriumBorder` cylinders were intersecting at identical Y coordinates.
  - Lowered `AtriumBorder` to `TopY = 1.8` as a stepped outer ring and raised `AtriumFloor` to `TopY = 2.1` with a clean `SmoothPlastic` antique finish to eliminate shimmering radial artifacts.
- Astral Astrolabe Visual Redesign:
  - Removed the solid rectangular gimbal block that was clipping through the central star sphere.
  - Built a four-tier fluted cylindrical pedestal with stepped marble and brass chalice capital.
  - Elevated the hovering neon StarCore to hover gracefully above the pedestal.
  - Replaced overwhelming default sparkles with a subtle celestial particle glow.
  - Added 3 animated orbital gold motes circling the central core.
- Dynamic Re-binding & Script Sync:
  - Updated `ServerScriptService.ObservatoryServer` with dynamic `DescendantAdded` detection to ensure ClickDetector and ProximityPrompt re-bind seamlessly across runtime updates.
  - Updated client controller in `StarterGui.ObservatoryHUD.ObservatoryController` to animate orbital motes in 3D.
