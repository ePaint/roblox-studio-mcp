# Internal Release v0.1.4

## Counter State Synchronization Fix & Complete UI Grid Overhaul

### Summary of Deliverables
- Counter & State Desync Bug Fix:
  - Eliminated the root cause of jumping and flickering counters: a surviving duplicate copy of `ObservatoryServer` in ServerScriptService that was independently tracking player currency and sending conflicting state sync packets every second.
  - Verified that exactly one authoritative `ObservatoryServer` script remains active. Purchases, clicks, and 1-second auto-gather loops now mutate a single synchronized data model.
- Dashboard 2-Column Grid Overhaul:
  - Replaced the wide stretched single-row spreadsheet layout with a balanced 2-column grid (`UIGridLayout`) of compact upgrade cards.
  - Eliminated the 600px empty dead void on cards by placing name and tier badge at the top, description in the middle, and cost alongside the purchase button at the bottom.
  - Fixed Tab Bar styling and order: forced logical ordering (`Lenses`, `Automation`, `Sanctum Wings`, `Guide`), and styled the selected tab with an illuminated Solarized Blue pill that cleanly stands out from the canvas.
- Compact, Snug Welcome Modal:
  - Resized the Welcome Modal to `460 x 260` studs, removing the massive blank gap and framing the bullet steps and entrance button with balanced padding.
- Ground & Spawn Clipping Fix:
  - Removed overlapping coplanar parts at the spawn point that were casting jagged triangular shadow artifacts onto the floor under the player.
  - Re-positioned lobby lamps and benches to snugly frame the new Z = 22 plaza.
  - Replaced lectern text with clean, high-resolution rendering.
